About the practice

Careful, modern dentistry — close to home

Dr. Mahjabin treats everyday dental problems — pain, cavities, broken teeth, missing teeth — with a calm, patient approach. Every visit is planned around your comfort, careful sterilisation and long-term oral health, not just the one procedure in front of her.

What we treat

Complete dental care, under one roof

From a simple checkup to a full implant — every treatment follows strict cleaning and safety steps to keep you safe.

Dental Implants

A new tooth root that looks and feels like your own. Dr. Mahjabin is specially trained in implant surgery.

Root Canal Treatment

Removes infection from inside a painful tooth so it can heal — done gently and precisely.

Crowns & Smile Repair

Tooth-coloured caps and fillings that fix damaged teeth and match your natural smile.

Extractions & Minor Surgery

Comfortable, careful removal of damaged or problem teeth, with clear aftercare guidance.

Orthodontics

Straightening and bite correction for both children and adults.

Family & General Dentistry

Cleaning, polishing and regular checkups for every member of the family.
